What to check before ordering

Save the listing URL and item ID with your shortlist so you can find the same listing again when comparing variants.

Match the size to your measurements

For shoes, use the seller’s chart for the exact model and check whether it lists foot length or insole length. For tops, compare chest width, body length and sleeve length with a garment you own.

Check the colour and QC photos

Confirm the chosen colour, size and included pieces. If your agent offers quality-control photos, request the views you need, such as both shoe size labels, a close-up of a print or a jacket zipper.

Compare the total cost to your door

Record the item price, domestic delivery, agent fees and estimated international shipping. Ask which parcel weight the shipping quote uses and check any applicable taxes or duties for your destination.

HooBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Where do the product links go?

Each product link opens its specific HipoBuyFind listing. Both buttons at the top open the HipoBuyFind homepage. This independent guide uses HipoBuyFind as its catalog source.

Can I use a listing with HooBuy?

Open the source listing and look for its original marketplace URL. Check whether your chosen agent accepts that URL and product for your destination before paying. The item IDs in this directory identify HipoBuyFind listings.

Does the displayed USD price include delivery?

The USD figure is the approximate item price shown by HipoBuyFind. Check the live listing for the selected variant and ask your agent for a separate shipping quote.
